	At the dawn of time – or at least as far back as we can trace – the first of all civilisations was founded: Al-Kashyyk, known today as the Almighty Empire, or simply the Empire. An order sustained by glory, perfection and honour, renowned for its very justice and reputation.

It was within its very bosom that its enemy was born. At the christening of its seventh successor and heir, a choice had to be made between the two princes.

 On the one hand, Rauru, the youngest, a wise young man whose heart was filled with as much zeal as respect for his elders, and who was the Emperor’s clear favourite; and on the other,

 Morgoth, the eldest, who, paradoxically, though older, was more power-hungry. 
The two young men had learnt to live together, and with age the differences between them had become much clearer. 

Rauru, though unfit for combat—the result of generations of inbreeding—possessed the gift of eloquence and selflessness, unlike his brother, who was more inclined towards a military career and mastery of the sword than towards princely duties. 

The Almighty’s decision was final, and the young man with the delicate complexion and golden curls was named heir to the throne, whilst the title of lord was bestowed upon the young Morgoth in return. The Order lasted no more than a few years and soon fell victim to a military coup led by the young ‘Lord of Fire’.

What followed was an all-out war between the two heirs to the throne. Known as the greatest massacre in this history, its consequences would be felt for centuries to come.

 The Lord of the Flame was defeated, sealed away and banished forever from the Holy Land, cast from the surface and buried alive beneath the earth, lost in the abyss.
